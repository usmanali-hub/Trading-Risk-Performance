# Executive Summary

## Objective

Evaluate trading performance and risk across strategies, instruments, sessions, and market regimes.

## Decision Lens

The analysis prioritizes consistency and risk-adjusted behavior rather than raw profit alone. A useful trading process should balance positive expectancy with controlled drawdowns and avoid excessive dependence on a small number of wins.

## Recommended Review Sequence

1. Confirm data quality and trade completeness.
2. Review total P&L and expectancy.
3. Compare profit factor and win/loss characteristics.
4. Inspect the equity curve and maximum drawdown.
5. Segment results by strategy, instrument, session, and regime.
6. Investigate any concentration or unstable periods before drawing conclusions.

## Data Caveat

All trade records are synthetic and created solely for portfolio demonstration. No live account performance is represented.
